# Reuters Live



Reuters Live is a video streaming service provided by Reuters, that enables 24/7 access to live news coverage on a wide range of topics, including global breaking news, sports, entertainment, and regional events.

The platform allows broadcasters, publishers, and digital media outlets to integrate high-definition, real-time streams directly into their broadcasts, social media channels, websites, and apps directly via API.

Users can access up to six concurrent live streams, offering real-time coverage of unfolding stories and significant events around the world​
